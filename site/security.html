Security & privacy

Your conversations stay yours

Zuno One is a shared inbox — your team needs to read and reply to messages, so the platform can process them. Everything below is what we do so that no one else can.

How message data is stored

Encrypted at rest

WhatsApp history and website-chat threads are sealed with AES‑256‑GCM before anything is written to disk, using a key derived per workspace. On the server’s disk and in every backup they are unreadable ciphertext. Set your own master key (kept off the server in a secret manager) so a stolen disk or leaked backup stays sealed.

Nothing warehoused from Meta or Google

Instagram, Messenger and Gmail messages are fetched live from Meta and Google each time an agent opens a thread. We don’t copy that history into our database.

The activity log keeps facts, not content

Reports and webhooks run off a lightweight event log that records that a message happened — channel, time, agent, counts — never the message body.

Tenant isolation

Every workspace’s data is scoped by tenant at the query layer. One customer’s data is never returned to another.

Access & accountability

Agents see only their queue

Channel-by-agent assignment means an agent’s inbox shows the conversations routed to them and nothing else.

Every action is attributed

Outgoing messages carry the agent’s name. Deletions record who deleted what. Assignments, status changes and automation runs are all logged.

Passwords hashed

Account passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes (cost 12), never in plain text. Sessions expire on a sliding 7‑day window.

Hardened by default

Security headers via Helmet, rate-limiting on login, locked-down CORS in production, and graceful handling of channel outages so the service stays up.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data

Your conversations and contacts are not sold, rented or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

We don’t train AI on your conversations

Optional AI features use the knowledge and settings you provide. Your customer conversations are not used to train shared models.

On the roadmap: independent penetration testing and a published report, SSO/SAML, configurable data-retention windows, and a signed DPA for every customer. Questions? legal@zunoone.com.
