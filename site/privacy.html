1. Who we are

Zuno One ("we", "us") operates the Zuno One platform. For personal data about our own account holders and website visitors, we are the controller. For personal data contained in the conversations and contacts our customers manage in the Service ("Customer Data"), our customer is the controller and we act as a processor on their instructions. Controller contact: legal@zunoone.com, [registered address].

2. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, work email, password (hashed), workspace name, role.
  • Billing data — plan, and limited details from our payment processor (Stripe); we do not store full card numbers.
  • Usage and device data — log data, IP address, browser/device info, pages and features used, timestamps, and diagnostic data.
  • Customer Data (as processor) — contact names, phone numbers, emails and social handles; message content pulled from connected channels for display; labels, notes, tags, reminders, appointments, survey responses, call logs, and website-widget conversations.
  • Support data — information you send us when you contact support.

3. How we use personal data (as controller)

  • To provide, secure, maintain and improve the Service.
  • To authenticate users and prevent abuse and fraud.
  • To bill and manage subscriptions.
  • To communicate with you about the Service, including service and security notices.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

Legal bases (GDPR): performance of a contract; our legitimate interests in running and securing the Service; consent where required (e.g. certain communications or cookies); and legal obligation.

4. Customer Data — our role as processor

We process Customer Data only to provide the Service and on the customer's documented instructions, as set out in these terms and any Data Processing Addendum. We do not sell Customer Data and do not use it to train models for other customers. If you are an individual whose data appears in a customer's workspace, please contact that business (the controller) to exercise your rights; we will assist them.

5. Sharing and sub-processors

We share personal data with service providers who help us run the Service, under contract and only as needed, including:

  • Cloud hosting — [hosting provider, e.g. Google Cloud], region [region].
  • Payments — Stripe.
  • AI model provider — [e.g. OpenAI], only if you enable AI features.
  • Channel APIs — Meta (Instagram, Messenger) and Google (Gmail) when you connect those channels.
  • Email / error monitoring — [providers].

We may also disclose data to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or in a merger or asset sale (with notice). A current sub-processor list is available on request.

6. International transfers

We and our providers may process data outside your country, including in [regions]. Where required we use appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Retention

We keep account and billing data for the life of the account and as needed for legal, tax and audit purposes. Customer Data is retained per the customer's configuration and deleted within a reasonable period after account termination unless law requires otherwise. Logs are kept for a limited period for security and debugging. Locally cached message text on the server (used to display recent history) is periodically pruned.

8. Security

We use technical and organisational measures including encryption in transit, hashed passwords, access controls, network isolation of the database, rate limiting and least-privilege access. No method is perfectly secure; we will notify affected customers and authorities of a personal data breach as required by law.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To exercise rights over data we hold as controller, email legal@zunoone.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. California residents: we do not sell or "share" personal information as defined by the CPRA.

10. Cookies

Our website and app use strictly necessary cookies (for login/session) and may use limited analytics. You can control cookies in your browser. [If you add non-essential analytics or marketing cookies, add a consent banner and describe them here.]

11. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy; material changes will be notified by email or in-app. The "last updated" date shows the current version.
