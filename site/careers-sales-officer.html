Sales Officer

Remote · ±5h CET Full-time Sales team Base + uncapped commission
Apply for this role

About the role

Zuno One has grown to 1,400+ teams mostly on inbound demo requests and word of mouth. We want our first dedicated Sales Officer to take ownership of that pipeline, add structured outbound to agencies and support teams, and turn interest into signed, well-fit customers.

You'll work directly with the founders and shape how we sell — the demo, the follow-up, the qualification, the handoff to Customer Success. There's no playbook yet; you'll write it.

What you'll do

  • Run product demos tailored to each prospect's channels and team size.
  • Manage the inbound pipeline from the website — respond fast, qualify well, keep it moving.
  • Do targeted outbound to agencies, e-commerce brands and support teams that would genuinely benefit.
  • Own deals end to end: first call, trial support, pricing, contract, close.
  • Feed patterns back to product — what prospects ask for, where we lose, what would unblock deals.
  • Keep the CRM tidy and the forecast honest.

What we're looking for

  • 2+ years in B2B SaaS sales, or a comparable role where you owned revenue outcomes.
  • Comfortable running a full cycle solo — prospecting through close.
  • Clear, friendly, concise written English; most of our selling happens in writing.
  • Self-directed in a remote, async team — you set your own week.
  • Genuine interest in the problem: customer support, messaging, small businesses and agencies.

Nice to have

  • Experience selling a support, CX or messaging product.
  • Worked at an agency, or sold to agencies.
  • Basic comfort with APIs and automation tools so you can speak to technical buyers.
  • A second language spoken in our top markets (German, French, Portuguese, Spanish).

Compensation & benefits

  • Competitive base salary plus uncapped commission on closed deals.
  • Annual profit share once you're past your first six months.
  • 5 weeks paid time off, plus local public holidays.
  • Learning budget and a home-office stipend.
  • Two paid team offsites a year.
