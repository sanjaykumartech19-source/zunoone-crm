FAQ

Pricing questions

What counts as a contact?
A contact is one person you've had a conversation with in the current billing period, across any channel. People you never message don't count, and the count resets each period.
What are AI credits?
One credit is roughly one AI-drafted reply or one autopilot response. Unused credits don't roll over. If you run out, AI features pause until the next period or an upgrade — the rest of Zuno One keeps working.
Can I change plans anytime?
Yes. Upgrades apply immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period so you keep what you've paid for.
Do you offer annual billing?
Yes — switch the toggle above. Billed once a year, the rate per month drops: Starter $70 (save $108/yr), Growth $146 (save $156/yr) and Advanced $239 (save $360/yr). Growth and Advanced also include the Project Management module free on annual (Advanced includes it on monthly too).
Is Project Management included?
It's included on Advanced (any billing cycle) and free on Growth when you're billed annually — on monthly Growth it isn't included. On Free or Starter you can buy it as an add-on: $20/mo, or $199/yr per workspace (2 months free). It's the full module either way — Board, List, Table, Calendar, Timeline and Dashboard views, subtasks, dependencies, custom fields, sprints, time tracking, recurring tasks, milestones, a portfolio Overview and per-member project permissions — all on the same customers and team as your inbox.
What happens when I hit a free-plan limit?
Nothing breaks. You keep full access to existing conversations; you're just prompted to upgrade before adding the seat or contact that would go over. Incoming messages always keep arriving.
Do you offer nonprofit or education discounts?
We do. Email sales@zunoone.com from your organisation's domain and we'll sort out a discounted plan.
