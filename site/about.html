Support teams deserve better tools

We started Zuno One in 2021 because juggling WhatsApp on a phone, Instagram on someone's laptop and a shared Gmail didn't scale past a handful of people. Today 1,400+ teams in 40 countries run their customer conversations on Zuno One — and we're still small, remote and profitable.

What we care about

Four things we won't compromise on

Answer fast

The product exists to shrink the time between a customer asking and a human answering. Every feature is judged against that.

Keep it simple

A new agent should be useful in ten minutes. We cut settings before we add them.

Earn trust

We store the minimum, we're clear about what we keep, and we never sell data. Your conversations are yours.

Stay independent

Profitable and self-funded, so we build for customers and the long term — not for a raise.
