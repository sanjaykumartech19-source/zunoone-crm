How billing, cancellations and refunds work on Zuno One paid plans.

1. Subscriptions

Paid Zuno One plans are billed in advance on a recurring monthly basis through Stripe. Your subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel.

2. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from Settings → Billing → Manage billing. When you cancel:

  • Your plan stays active until the end of the current paid period.
  • You are not billed again.
  • At period end the workspace moves to the Free plan; data above Free-plan limits may become read-only.

3. Refunds

Payments are non-refundable and we do not provide partial refunds or credits for unused time, a downgrade mid-cycle, or periods where you did not use the Service. As a goodwill exception, if you contact support@zunoone.com within [14] days of your first paid charge and have not substantially used paid features, we may at our discretion refund that charge.

[If you offer annual billing: state whether it is refundable. Typical policy — annual plans are paid up front and are non-refundable; you may cancel to stop the next annual renewal.]

5. Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we retry and mark the workspace past due. If payment is not resolved within [7] days the subscription is cancelled and the workspace moves to the Free plan. Reactivating requires a successful payment.

6. Price changes

We give at least 30 days' notice of a price increase; it takes effect at your next renewal. If you do not agree, cancel before that renewal.

7. Taxes

Prices exclude VAT / GST / sales tax, which is added where applicable based on your billing details.

8. Chargebacks

If you have a billing problem, contact us first — we resolve most issues quickly. Initiating a chargeback without contacting us may result in immediate suspension of the workspace.

9. Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing and has no refund implications. We may change its limits or discontinue it with notice.
