Security & privacy

Your conversations stay yours

We built the storage layer so a stolen disk, a leaked backup or a curious engineer can't read your customers' messages.

Encrypted at rest

WhatsApp history and website chats are sealed with AES‑256 using a key unique to your workspace. On disk and in backups they're unreadable ciphertext.

Nothing warehoused

Instagram, Messenger and Gmail messages are pulled live from Meta and Google when you open a thread — never copied onto our servers.

Never sold, never trained on

We don't sell your data and we don't use your conversations to train AI models. Your workspace data is isolated from every other tenant.

Every action is attributed

Agents only see the queue assigned to them. Outgoing messages carry the agent's name, and deletions record who deleted what.
